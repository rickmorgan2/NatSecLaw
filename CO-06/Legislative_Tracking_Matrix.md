**H.R. 8707 (No Funds for Iran War Act)** was introduced by Rep. Pat Ryan, with Crow as an original cosponsor. The original PDF brief described Crow as having introduced the measure; the separate Markdown brief corrects this. Similarly, **H.R. 7016 (No Funds for NATO Invasion Act)** was introduced by Rep. William Keating, with Crow as an original cosponsor.

## Interpretation

A bill reaching a committee or being ordered reported is not equivalent to enactment. For NDAAs, the enacted law is directly verified; attribution of discrete provisions to an individual member requires comparing final statutory language with amendments or committee materials. Initial bill-text links document sponsorship and committee referrals; a bill can receive later committee action without a new GPO introduced version.
