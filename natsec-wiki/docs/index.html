U.S. National Security Legislation

A working wiki and study guide to the statutes that organize, authorize, and limit American national security policy, with the foreign policy laws they connect to.

87 statutes, 167 recorded connections, 11 study units, and 2 member lenses. Every statute page links to its official text on GovInfo; the Sources page lists everything the wiki draws on.

New here? The study guide explains how these laws fit together, from the Constitution down to single code sections, and walks through them in eleven units. The knowledge graph shows the same connections visually.
