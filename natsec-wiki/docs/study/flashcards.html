Flashcards

Every statute, court case, and key term, with spaced review. Tap or click a card to flip it.

How review works. Each card sits in one of five boxes. Marking a card I know this moves it up a box and schedules it further out: 1, 3, 7, 14, then 30 days. Still learning sends it back to the first box, due again tomorrow. Tick Only cards due for review for a daily session. Your record is saved in this browser only.

On a phone, swipe the card left or right to move. On a keyboard, space flips and the arrow keys move.

Independent reference project. Not affiliated with any congressional office. Not legal advice; check citations against the linked official text.
